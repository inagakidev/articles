## What he4rt ink is

A space of the He4rt community to publish and read technical articles. Reading doesn't need an account. To write, comment and like, you need an account with a confirmed email.

## Your account

- To create an account, you declare you are 16 or older.
- Your username is public and shows on your profile and your articles.
- Accounts that don't confirm their email within 7 days are deleted.
- You can delete your account whenever you want.

## Publishing

- Every article is reviewed before it's published.
- Content that breaks these Terms may be rejected, unpublished or reported.
- Anyone who breaks the rules may have their account suspended or banned, with the right to appeal.
