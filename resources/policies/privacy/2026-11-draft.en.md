> **Draft version.** This text summarizes how the platform handles your data today and will be replaced by the final version before launch.

## What we keep

- Name, username, email and password (stored only as a hash).
- The language you chose for the site and the emails.
- The date and version of the Terms and of this Policy that you accepted.
- Security events of your account, such as login blocks and password and email changes, with IP and browser.

We don't keep your date of birth: at sign-up, you only declare you are 16 or older.

## Cookies

We only use essential cookies: the session, the theme and the language.

## For how long

- Suspicious login attempts: 90 days.
- Accounts that don't confirm their email: 7 days.
- Other periods follow the platform's retention table and will be detailed in the final version.

## Emails

We send emails about your account, such as the sign-up confirmation and security notices. The sending service will be named in the final version.
